Read `src/app.md`, then the note in `src/host_notes/` that matches the host you
are running under. `src/host_notes/README.md` lists them.

- **A session that will only read is exempt from both**, and from the identity
  and the queue they lead to — a scheduled briefing, a lookup, a question
  answered out of a file. Read what was asked for, answer, and stop.
- **A session that turns out to need a write starts over at the top of this
  file**, before the write. The exemption is what a session does, never what it
  decided about itself at the start.
