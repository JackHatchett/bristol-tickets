# story-proposals

A story or world change proposed by the user, by a session's own reasoning in
`src/skills/prose-drafting/SKILL.md`, or by a flag raised in
`src/skills/manuscript-review/SKILL.md`. The notebook half of it runs on
`src/skills/notebook-proposal/SKILL.md`.

## The two homes

- **Worldbuilding and story** — world, characters, plot, lore, scenes, tone. The
  notebook, per `src/skills/notebook-proposal/SKILL.md`.
- **Voice** — the author-voice profile, technique cards, lexicon. Captured via
  `src/skills/voice-distillation/SKILL.md` and `tools/writing_tools/voice_capture.md` under the
  provenance firewall. A proposed voice fact takes the notebook route too.

Reconciliation spans the wiki plus the active project's content-rules file.
