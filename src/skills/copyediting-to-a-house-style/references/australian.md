# australian — Australian Government Style Manual, with the Macquarie Dictionary

Builds on: new_harts

Australian publishing in the style of the *Australian Government Style Manual*
(digital edition, stylemanual.gov.au), with the *Macquarie Dictionary* for
spelling. The Style Manual shares British conventions for quotation marks,
dashes and dates, set out in `new_harts.md`, and departs from them at the
points below, which replace or add to the rules of `new_harts.md` for this
style. Each rule here is stated in this file's own words and carries the ID a
flag cites. The Style Manual itself is the authority where a case falls
outside them, and a flag on such a case says so.

Sources: the Style Manual's pages on commas, dashes, quotation marks,
choosing numerals or words, ordinal numbers, percentages, dates and time,
spelling, contractions, and Latin shortened forms, at stylemanual.gov.au.

## Replacing rules of new_harts

- **AUS-P1 No serial comma** (replaces NHR-P1). In a list of three or more, no
  comma before the final *and* or *or*: *bread, salt and wine*. A comma goes
  there only where the last item itself joins two words with *and*, or where
  leaving it out would misread.
- **AUS-P3 Ranges in words** (replaces NHR-P3). In running text a span takes
  *from … to* or *between … and*. An unspaced en dash is kept for financial and
  calendar years (*2024–25*), lifespans, page spans and limited space.
- **AUS-N1 Words or numerals** (replaces NHR-N1). *Zero* and *one* in words;
  2 and above in numerals. Words at the start of a sentence (or the sentence
  recast), for fractions (*two-thirds*), and for ordinals *first* to *ninth*;
  numerals from *10th*. In dialogue, a number the author wrote in words is a
  voice choice and is queried rather than corrected.
- **AUS-N3 Centuries and decades** (replaces NHR-N3). Centuries in numerals,
  *century* lowercase (*the 21st century*, *the 3rd century*); decades in
  numerals with no apostrophe (*the 1990s*).
- **AUS-N4 Times** (replaces NHR-N4). Numerals, a colon between hours and
  minutes, *am* and *pm* lowercase with no points after a non-breaking space
  (*7:30 am*, *4 pm*). *Noon*, *midday* or *midnight* rather than *12 pm* or
  *12 am*; never *am* or *pm* beside *morning*, *afternoon* or *night*.
- **AUS-N6 Percentages** (replaces NHR-N6). The symbol after a numeral (*55%*);
  *per cent*, two words, where the number is in words, as at the start of a
  sentence (*Fifty-five per cent*). *Percent* as one word is a departure.
- **AUS-H3 Spelling** (replaces NHR-H3). The first headword the *Macquarie
  Dictionary* gives. British forms (*colour*, *centre*, *grey*, *travelled*),
  *-ise* and *-isation* (*realise*, *organisation*), *-yse* (*analyse*), and
  *program* for every sense.
- **AUS-H4 Shortened forms** (replaces NHR-H4). No full stops in contractions,
  abbreviations, acronyms or initialisms (*Dr*, *Mr*, *Prof*, *St*, *US*,
  *UK*). *e.g.* and *i.e.* keep their full stops and take no comma after them.
  Initials in a name take neither stops nor spaces (*William LP Sanders*).
